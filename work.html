01 / Reporting & spreadsheets

Make a report
ready for review.

Professional workflow
with anonymized excerpt

Tools used
Google Sheets, GA4, Search Console, Ubersuggest, Looker Studio and Brevo
Outputs
Weekly SEO updates, monthly reviews, comparison tables and email summaries

The challenge

Search, website and email figures need the right dates and campaign context before they can support a recommendation. A change in a spreadsheet is a starting point for review.

My responsibility

I reviewed source data, checked comparisons, investigated unusual changes and prepared concise findings and follow-up actions for agency client reporting.

Work completed

  1. Confirmed the source, date range and comparison period.
  2. Organized ranking, traffic and campaign information in reports and spreadsheets.
  3. Checked unusual changes and separated observations from possible explanations.
  4. Wrote an update with the finding, relevant limitation and next action.

02 / Operations & coordination

Give recurring tasks
a clear handoff.

Professional workflow
with portfolio template

Tools used
Basecamp, Google Docs and Sheets, Slack, ClickUp and Otter.ai
Outputs
Task updates, process guidance, documented follow-ups and quality reviews

The challenge

Recurring marketing tasks involve different sources, deadlines and outstanding inputs. A status update needs enough detail for another person to continue the work.

My responsibility

At SEO Leads, I coordinated content and campaign tasks, documented processes, tracked follow-ups and reviewed deliverables for accuracy.

Work completed

  1. Checked the required output, service, location, dates and source references.
  2. Organized recurring work and recorded owners, due dates and missing inputs.
  3. Prepared process guides and communicated the next action.
  4. Reviewed date consistency, keyword and URL relevance, and duplicate records before handoff.

Inspect the handoff structure

What a usable task record includes

Source and task
Where the request came from and what needs to be done.
Owner and due date
Who is handling the work and the agreed timing.
Status and blocker
What is complete and what input is still needed.
Review and next action
What was checked and how to continue.

Blank portfolio template based on the documented coordination workflow. No internal task board or client tracker is reproduced.

Result and evidence

The work produced documented task handoffs, recurring updates and process guidance. The template makes the handoff fields inspectable. No reduction in turnaround time or missed deadlines is claimed.

03 / Research, content & websites

Move research
toward publication.

Professional workflow
with anonymized excerpt

Tools used
Ubersuggest, Search Console, Google Sheets and Docs; WordPress and Squarespace in website work
Outputs
Keyword categories, content direction, revisions and approved website posts

The challenge

A keyword list needs a clear purpose, and draft content needs review before publication. Research, client feedback and website updates have to stay connected.

My responsibility

My agency work included keyword research and categorization, content preparation, written client communication, requested revisions and publishing approved posts.

Work completed

  1. Reviewed the business offer and service area before organizing topics.
  2. Grouped searches by service and intent, then mapped them to appropriate pages.
  3. Prepared blog material and coordinated client review and approval.
  4. Implemented requested changes and published the approved content.
